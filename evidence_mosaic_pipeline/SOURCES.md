# Official Source Policy

Use only official/public government release material as source imagery for this project.

Approved starting points:

- DOJ Epstein Library: https://www.justice.gov/epstein
- DOJ press release on the 3.5 million page publication, including the 180,000+ image reference: https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/department-justice-publishes-35-million-responsive-pages-compliance-epstein-files
- House Oversight / official committee releases linked from official House or DOJ pages, including the House disclosures linked from the DOJ Epstein Library.

Do not use:

- YouTube, Twitter/X, Reddit, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, or other social media reposts.
- News article screenshots or image crops.
- Third-party archives, mirrors, torrents, or compilations.
- Images where provenance cannot be traced back to an official release.

Image handling rules:

- Use only neutral/mundane evidence material: interiors, exteriors, landscapes, objects, and other non-sensitive scene photos.
- Skip faces, passports, IDs, readable addresses, explicit/graphic content, and obvious personal identifying information.
- Do not add names, accusations, labels, captions, or claims about people in the images.
- Treat the images only as visual mosaic texture material.

Downloader note:

`build_mosaic.py --download-official` is best-effort and intentionally limited to official allowed hosts. It is not meant to bypass age gates, captchas, JavaScript search tools, or access controls. When the official site is dynamic, manually download official images or archives and place them in `source_images/`.
